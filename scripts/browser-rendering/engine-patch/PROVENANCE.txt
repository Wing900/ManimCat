Why the patch exists
--------------------
The pinned engine's web.rs exports only `init` and `Player`. `Player.draw` composites the
player face (controls, timeline, help, captions, status) over the film, and `film` only
reports [width, height]. A canvas screenshot therefore cannot be a clean image and cannot be
made clean by cropping without losing scene content.

The patch adds `Player.render_image(width, height)`:
- shows the finished take's closing frame (the frame recorded after `Scene.tear_down`);
- encodes the take's film views at exactly the requested take size;
- copies the film color texture into a dedicated MAP_READ buffer, never the surface;
- maps that buffer asynchronously and returns raw RGBA bytes;
- retries if the see-through lists overflowed, using the engine's existing overflow path.

It changes no existing export, video draw path, native runner, sandbox or narration timing.
The generated engine.js/engine_bg.wasm are build outputs and remain outside Git.
